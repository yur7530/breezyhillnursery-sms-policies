SMS Privacy Policy

Breezy Hill Nursery, Inc. respects your privacy.

We collect mobile phone numbers from employees who voluntarily opt in to receive one-to-one, non-marketing SMS messages for internal business communications, including IT support, system notifications, and operational alerts.

Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent data are not shared with third parties.

Employees may opt out at any time by replying STOP or request assistance by replying HELP.
