Privacy Policy

Brand: Breezy Hill Nursery, Inc.
Effective date: February 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Breezy Hill Nursery, Inc. (“Breezy Hill,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information, including employee mobile phone numbers used for one-to-one, non-marketing SMS communications for internal business purposes.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact information: name, work email address, department, and mobile phone number.
  • Employment-related identifiers: employee ID or similar internal identifiers.
  • Communications data: records of operational communications (e.g., message content, timestamps).
  • Device/network information: limited technical data if you access internal systems (e.g., IP address), where applicable.

How We Use Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Provide internal IT support and respond to employee requests.
  • Send system updates, account notifications, and operational alerts.
  • Maintain security of our systems and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Administer employee communications and related recordkeeping.

SMS Communications

If you opt in, Breezy Hill Nursery, Inc. may send one-to-one, non-marketing SMS messages for internal IT and operational communications (such as IT support, system updates, and account notifications). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out: You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Help: Reply HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not required to make a purchase. (This program is intended for internal employee communications.)

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share personal information only as needed to operate our business, such as:

  • Service providers: vendors that help deliver communications or operate IT systems (e.g., messaging/telecommunications providers, cloud hosting providers) acting on our behalf and under appropriate contractual protections.
  • Legal and compliance: when required to comply with law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Security and safety: to protect the rights, property, or safety of Breezy Hill, employees, or others.

Mobile phone numbers collected for SMS communications are not shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including operational, security, and legal compliance needs.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • SMS opt-out: Reply STOP to any SMS message to opt out.
  • Update your information: You may request updates or corrections to your contact information through internal HR/IT processes.
